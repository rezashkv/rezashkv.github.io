I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Dr. Heng Huang.

I work on making large models adaptive: models that understand their own limitations, select the best internal or external expert, use their failures to select or generate what to learn next, and adapt efficiently and safely when data is scarce.
